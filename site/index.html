What it does

Your chief of staff

Say what you need in plain words. It hands the work to the right agent, starts a new one when nothing fits, and tells you when it's done.

Agents in parallel

Many agents working at once, each on its own task, so you build and ship much faster. See at a glance which are working, done or waiting on you.

Spaces for every project

Group agents by project, fold away what you're not working on, and pick up right where you left off.

One window, every provider

Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Copilot, OpenCode and more on your computer, plus Cursor, Copilot, Jules and Claude agents in the cloud, all in one place.

Never miss a question

When an agent needs you, answer in one click, at your desk or on your phone. Pair it free with a Cloudflare account or Tailscale, end-to-end encrypted.

Review and ship

See what each agent changed, leave notes, and send the work out from the same window when it's ready.

All your computers

Link your other machines, Mac, Windows or Linux, and switch between them in one window, each with its own agents. A phone paired with one reaches them all.

Meeting notes

It notices when a call starts and offers to take notes: your mic and the call, transcribed as you talk, with what was on the shared screen. A summary with decisions and action items when it ends.

Every account

Several Claude or Codex accounts side by side, each named your way. Hit a limit? Carry the conversation on in another account.

Install

macOS, Linuxcurl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sahil1/bridge/main/install.sh | sh
Windowsirm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sahil1/bridge/main/install.ps1 | iex

Paste it in Terminal (or PowerShell on Windows). It downloads the app, installs it and opens it. Run it again to update.

Downloaded the Mac app instead? It's signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without a warning. On Windows, SmartScreen may ask first: More info › Run anyway.

On your Android phone

Download for Android (.apk)
  1. Allow installs from your browser or Files: when Android says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source.
  2. Open Bridge-AI-android.apk and tap Install.
  3. Open Bridge AI and scan the QR code from your computer: Settings › Devices › Pair a device.

It isn't on Google Play yet. To update, install a newer APK over it; your computers stay paired. Its SHA-256 is in the release's SHA256SUMS.txt. With no computer yet, it has Try the demo and Cloud Agents.

Yours, on your machine

Bridge AI runs on your computer. Your agents use their own sign-ins, and the project collects nothing about you: no account, no analytics, no ads. The code is all on GitHub if you'd rather read it first or run it yourself.
